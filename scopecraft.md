# Scopecraft

Scopecraft is a design language for a proposal-builder SaaS, from discovery call to SOW. It comes from a concept by Nizam (https://x.com/nizamdesign/status/2106904954581692578). Light and neutral: near-black primary buttons, soft white cards on a pale gray page, tiny uppercase labels, and color only for status.

## Rules

1. One near-black primary button per view. Everything else is outlined.
2. Color means status only: green up or approved, red down or blocked, amber needs attention, blue is AI or source information.
3. Every card says what it is in a small uppercase label.
4. Numbers use tabular digits and carry a delta chip when there is a comparison.
5. Show the reason next to each decision: why it is asked, what it changes.
