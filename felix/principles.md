# Principles

Felix treats money as an act of care between families. Four ideas, one intent. The product stays human, not clinical.

## 01 · Warm, not corporate

A warm white canvas, rounded corners, living turquoise. The product feels like a compañero, not a bank branch.

## 02 · Radically transparent

Every fee is a visible line item — even when it is zero. We never surprise with costs.

## 03 · Clear in two languages

Spanish-first, jargon-free, informal *tú* always. English only for international or B2B contexts.

## 04 · Accessible by default

WCAG AA contrast, always-visible focus, built on Radix primitives with correct ARIA.
