Core coverage: buttons/groups, dropdowns, pagination, alerts, toasts, badges, progress, spinners, placeholders, cards, accordion, breadcrumb, tabs, lists, tables, forms, modal, tooltip, popover, carousel, close, collapse, navbar/footer, offcanvas, scrollspy, notifications, attachment/file, date picker, choice card, stepper, navigation menu, sidebar/footer, hover card, message, range, input group, validation, error/retry, empty state, select, checks/radio/switch, avatars.

Extended coverage follows the Felix/Lumen variant checklist. Samples are local, not a production application or backend. Native dialogs contain focus and support Escape. Lightweight menus and tabs demonstrate appearance/state but applications still own routing and full composite-widget keyboard behavior. Files remain local, messages stay drafts, success labels do not indicate a real operation.

Shared motion covers entry, controls and observed local state updates. Motion pages cover persistent card movement, status, expansion and stagger; the new labs also cover keyed reorder/add/remove. Reduced motion preserves content and final states without animation.
