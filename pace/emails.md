# Pace emails

Four transactional templates: booking confirmed, class reminder, payment receipt and credits expiring. Switch the dark mail client toggle to check both appearances.

<PaceEmails />

## Rules

- 520px table layout, inline styles, system font stack. No web fonts or background images are required.
- One primary button per email, at least 44px tall, text contrast above 4.5:1 in light and dark.
- Subject states the action and the time. Keep preheader text under 90 characters.
- Footer says why the person got the email and how to change notifications.
- Samples use fictitious data and are never sent.
